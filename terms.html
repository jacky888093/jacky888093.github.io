Terms of Service

Terms for using the personal Jacky YouTube Studio Windows application.

Personal use

Use the local application to organize your own video files, prepare publishing information, and submit selected videos to a YouTube channel you are authorized to manage. Jacky YouTube Studio is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or YouTube.

YouTube rules

When using the application with YouTube, follow the YouTube Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, the YouTube API Services Terms of Service, and applicable law.

Your publishing decisions

Before you approve a submission, confirm that you have the rights and permissions to upload the video and that the selected Google account and YouTube channel are correct. Review the video, titles, description, audience, disclosures, visibility, and release time. This includes reviewing any suggested or AI-assisted copy.

A local draft or dry run does not publish a video. YouTube controls processing, availability, and the final status of uploads and scheduled releases.

Privacy and authorization

See the Privacy Policy for local storage, Google authorization, and how to revoke access.
